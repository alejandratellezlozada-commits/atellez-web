Dr. Alejandra Téllez took part as a speaker at an international dermatology congress, representing Colombia and sharing her clinical experience in advanced aesthetic procedures with an audience of specialists from many countries.

## Colombian dermatology on the international stage

Dr. Téllez's participation in international forums reflects the level of Colombian dermatology and its standing in the global context of the specialty. In her presentation, she covered topics such as:

- Advanced injection techniques with botulinum toxin and hyaluronic acid
- Clinical experience with exosomes in alopecia and skin regeneration
- Combination treatment protocols for natural-looking results

## A two-way exchange of knowledge

For Dr. Téllez, taking part in international conferences is not only an opportunity to share, but also to learn. Contact with colleagues from other parts of the world brings trends, protocols and advances that have not yet reached Latin America.

> "Presenting at an international congress forces you to be very rigorous with your clinical evidence. And listening to colleagues from other countries gives you a perspective you can't get any other way."

Experiences like this feed directly into her clinical practice, benefiting patients looking for world-class treatments in Bogotá.
