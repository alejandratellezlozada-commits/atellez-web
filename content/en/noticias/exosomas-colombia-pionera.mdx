Dr. Alejandra Téllez has become one of the first dermatologists in Colombia to incorporate exosome therapy into her clinical practice, both for skin treatments and for alopecia.

## What are exosomes?

Exosomes are extracellular vesicles of biological origin that carry proteins, growth factors and messenger RNA between cells. In dermatology, they can stimulate tissue regeneration more precisely and effectively than conventional treatments.

Unlike PRP, exosomes act directly on the cellular repair mechanisms, without the need to draw the patient's blood.

## Clinical applications in the office

At Dr. Téllez's practice, exosomes are currently used for:

- **Androgenetic alopecia:** combined with scalp mesotherapy to boost follicle reactivation
- **Facial rejuvenation:** after laser procedures, to speed up recovery and improve skin quality
- **Acne scars:** in combination with other resurfacing treatments

## Training and scientific backing

Dr. Téllez has trained in the use of exosomes through international programs and closely follows the scientific evidence supporting their use. Her position is clear: adopt new technologies only when the evidence justifies it, and always under a rigorous medical protocol.
