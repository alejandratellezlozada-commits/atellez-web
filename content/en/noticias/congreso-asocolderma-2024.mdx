Dr. Alejandra Téllez took an active part in the 2024 National Congress of the Colombian Association of Dermatology and Dermatologic Surgery (AsoColDerma), one of the most important scientific events for the specialty in the country.

## Top-level scientific updates

The congress brought together dermatologists from across the country, along with international specialists, presenting the latest advances in clinical and aesthetic dermatology and laser technology.

Dr. Téllez used the opportunity to update her knowledge in:

- New biostimulation techniques with botulinum toxin
- Treatment protocols for androgenetic alopecia
- Advances in exosome and PRP therapy
- Management of severe acne with residual scarring

## A commitment to continuing education

For Dr. Téllez, taking part in scientific conferences is a fundamental part of practicing medicine responsibly. Staying up to date with the evidence makes it possible to offer patients the most effective and safest treatments available.

> "In dermatology, advances happen very fast. Taking part in these events isn't optional: it's an obligation to our patients."

As an active member of AsoColDerma, Dr. Téllez maintains a permanent connection with the Colombian dermatology community and the specialty's international standards.
