If hyaluronic acid is the go-to filler for restoring facial volume immediately, Sculptra is the option for those who want a more gradual, natural and long-lasting result. It isn't a conventional filler: it's a biostimulator, which means it works by stimulating your own body to produce more collagen.

## What is Sculptra?

Sculptra is a product based on **poly-L-lactic acid (PLLA)**, a synthetic biodegradable polymer that has been used in medicine for decades — it's the same material used in absorbable sutures. When injected into the deep dermis, it acts as a scaffold that stimulates fibroblasts (the cells that produce collagen) to generate new tissue.

The result isn't immediate: the body takes several weeks to build that new collagen. But once it does, the effect is long-lasting — up to 2 years — and completely natural, because it's your own collagen that rejuvenates the skin.

## What is Sculptra used for?

### Loss of facial volume

With age, the face loses volume in several areas: cheeks, temples, jawline, around the mouth. Sculptra restores that volume progressively, without the "puffy" look that excessive conventional fillers can sometimes create.

### Moderate skin laxity

By stimulating collagen production, Sculptra improves the skin's overall firmness. It doesn't replace a surgical facelift, but for moderate laxity the results are very satisfying.

### Deep wrinkles and folds

Nasolabial folds, marionette lines and deep forehead wrinkles respond well to Sculptra when combined with other techniques.

### Rejuvenating the neck, décolletage and hands

Sculptra isn't only used on the face. It is excellent for treating neck laxity, improving the appearance of the décolletage (fine horizontal lines) and rejuvenating the hands.

## Who is the ideal candidate?

Sculptra is especially suitable for:

- People aged 40 to 60 with loss of facial volume and moderate laxity
- Patients who prefer gradual, natural results (not drastic immediate changes)
- People who want durability: not having to repeat treatment every 6–8 months
- Candidates for anti-aging prevention from age 35–40

It isn't the best option if you're looking for a visible result within days (for example, before an upcoming event).

## What is the protocol?

Sculptra is usually applied in **2–3 sessions** spaced 4–6 weeks apart. This spacing is part of the treatment design: each session builds on the previous one.

The injection technique varies: conventional needles or cannulas can be used, depending on the area and the physician's preference. The procedure takes about 30–45 minutes.

## When are results visible?

This is the key difference from HA: Sculptra's results are **gradual**.

- **Immediately after:** you'll see some volume from the carrier liquid (sterile water), but it disappears within days
- **4–6 weeks:** collagen stimulation starts to show
- **3–6 months:** full, natural results
- **Duration:** 18–24 months or more, depending on the case

## Aftercare

Post-Sculptra care has one important feature: **massage**. It is recommended to massage the treated area for 5 minutes, 5 times a day, for 5 days (the "5-5-5" rule). This distributes the product evenly and reduces the risk of small nodules forming.

Other care:
- Avoid intense exercise for the first 24 hours
- No sauna or intense sun exposure for 1 week
- Use SPF 50+

## Sculptra vs. hyaluronic acid: which should you choose?

They aren't mutually exclusive — in fact, they complement each other very well. In general terms:

| | Sculptra | Hyaluronic Acid |
|---|---|---|
| Type of result | Gradual, natural | Immediate |
| Duration | 18–24 months | 6–18 months depending on area |
| Mechanism | Biostimulation (your own collagen) | Direct filler |
| Ideal for | Overall volume, laxity | Specific areas (lips, under-eyes, nose) |
| Reversible | No (degrades gradually) | Yes (with hyaluronidase) |

In many patients we use both in the same treatment plan: Sculptra to restore overall volume and firmness, and HA for specific areas that need immediate definition.
